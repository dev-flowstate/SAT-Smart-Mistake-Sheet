Part 1 · Once per file

Turn on the buttons

Windows marks files from email, WhatsApp or websites as untrusted, and Excel switches the buttons off. Clear the mark once and it stays cleared.

1

Open the file’s Properties

Close Excel first. In your Downloads folder, right-click SAT_Mistake_Log_v11.xlsm and choose Properties.

2

Tick Unblock, then OK

At the bottom of the General tab. No Unblock box? The file is already fine.

3

Open the file and click Enable Content

Double-click the file and click Enable Content on the yellow bar. Excel remembers this for the file.

Seeing a different bar?

Yellow “PROTECTED VIEW” — Excel opened it read-only. Click Enable Editing.

Red “SECURITY RISK — blocked macros” — there is no button on this bar, which is normal. Step 2 was skipped: close Excel and do steps 1 and 2.

Is it safe?

Excel warns about every file with macros. These only move the buttons, show menus and place pictures — no internet, no other programs, no file access. The code is on GitHub.

Not on Windows?

Use SAT_Mistake_Log_v11_NoMacros.xlsx. Pick values with the arrow at the cell edge and add pictures with Insert › Pictures.

Not Google Sheets

Uploading to Google Sheets breaks the Section–Topic link. Use Excel on Windows or Mac.

Part 2 · Logging a mistake

Log a mistake

One row per question you got wrong. Almost every column is a list, so there is very little typing.

4

Click a cell, then Choose

Under Section, Topic, Test, Module, Mistake Type, Priority or Status, a dark Choose ▼ button appears.

5

Click an option

It goes into the cell. (clear this cell) at the bottom empties it.

6

Pick the Section first

The Topic list follows it: Reading & Writing shows its 11 skills, Math shows its 19.

If the Topic list is empty, that row has no Section yet.

7

Q # — type only the number

Type 12, not “Q12”. Fill in Module too: numbers restart in Module 2.

8

Priority and Status colour themselves

High/Not Reviewed red, Medium/Reviewing yellow, Low/Mastered green.

Write useful notes

Mistake: what you did. Why: one level deeper. Avoid: an action — “circle what the question asks”, not “be careful”.

Find rows fast

The arrow on each blue heading is a filter. Before a test, filter Priority to High and Status to Not Reviewed.

What is locked

Type in rows 5–304. The title, headings, dashboard, instructions and lists are locked against accidents.

Part 3 · Question pictures

Add, zoom, replace or remove a picture

Seeing the original question again is what makes the review work. Each row holds one picture, saved inside the file.

9

Copy a screenshot of the question

Press ⊞ Win+Shift+S and drag a box around the question in Bluebook. It is copied, not saved.

10

Click the cell and press Ctrl + V

Click the row’s Question Screenshot cell, press Ctrl+V — or click Add Photo to pick a file.

11

It shrinks to fit the cell

It moves with its row when you sort, and stays in the file if you email it.

12

Use the Photo menu

Click a screenshot cell that has a picture 1 — its button says Photo ▼. Click it:

  • 2Zoom In — shows it large
  • 3Replace Photo… — pick another image
  • 4Remove Photo — deletes it
13

Put a zoomed picture back

Click Close, or click the big picture itself.

Part 4 · Studying

Use the Dashboard to decide what to study

Click the Dashboard tab at the bottom of Excel. It updates by itself as you log.

Your Top Repeat Offenders ranks the topics you miss most often. Work down that list, top first — that is where your points are.

Each bar counts logged mistakes. A longer bar is a weaker area.

A good weekly routine

Log straight after each practice test, then pick the top two Repeat Offenders, redo those questions from your screenshots, and set them to Mastered once you get them right twice.
